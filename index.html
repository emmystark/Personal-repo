        When the day moves

        1. Blocks are portable. Miss 5:30, run it after you get home. It counts if the minutes land before midnight.
        2. Daily minimum is 3 hours, 2.5 on Friday. Below that, put the gap into Sunday's catch up slot.
        3. Never drop the 5:30 block two days running. That block carries the hard calculation work.
        4. Commute is capped at 1 hour. Home by 2 pm? Start the evening block early and sleep earlier.
        5. Lights out by 11:30. Sleep is when the reading sticks.
        6. The week before a test, every free block goes to that course. Send Claude the date and the plan gets rebuilt.
